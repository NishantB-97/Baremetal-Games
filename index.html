JETFALL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the game JETFALL ("the game") handles. The game is published by Bare Metal Games.

Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts and no servers. Your progress, settings and in-game credits are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Local (LAN) multiplayer

When you host or join a LAN match, your device exchanges game data (player name, positions and actions) directly with other devices on the same local network. This data is not sent to the internet and is not stored after the match.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), and how you interact with ads. This is governed by Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
